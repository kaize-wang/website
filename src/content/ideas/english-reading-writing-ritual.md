## The thought

Language practice should attach itself to work I already care about. A short English summary after reading a paper may be more sustainable than maintaining a separate vocabulary system.

## The smallest useful version

Read normally, close the paper, write a short explanation from memory, then compare wording against the source only after the first draft exists.
