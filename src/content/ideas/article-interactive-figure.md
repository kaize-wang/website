## The thought

The best interaction should feel inseparable from the explanation around it. A generic sandbox asks the reader to learn a tool; a good interactive figure answers one concrete question in the paragraph where that question appears.

## Constraint

Do not build an interaction just because it looks impressive. The trigger should be a real explanatory bottleneck.
