## The thought

I want ideas to have somewhere to live before they are polished enough to become notes, projects, or research outputs.

## The key distinction

Notes are for things I have understood well enough to preserve. Ideas are for things I may want to investigate, build, write, or try.

## The experiment

The first version should prioritize three actions: capture, choose, and advance. If the dashboard encourages sorting for its own sake, it is too complicated.
