I wanted a place that sits somewhere between an academic homepage and a private notebook.

A conventional homepage is useful for identity. It tells people who I am, where I study, what I work on, and how to contact me. But it does not leave much room for the unfinished parts of research: questions that are still unclear, papers that changed how I think, derivations that took several attempts, or small technical ideas that are worth preserving.

This site is meant to hold both.

## Research notes

Some posts will be close to my current work on inventory systems, warranty, stochastic optimization, and operations research. They may begin as working notes rather than polished papers.

## Reading notes

I also want a durable record of papers I read carefully. The goal is not to summarize everything, but to keep the parts that genuinely change my understanding.

## Writing over time

The categories may change as the site grows. I would rather let the archive reveal what I actually care about than design a perfect taxonomy in advance.

For now, the simplest rule is enough: if an idea is worth remembering, it is worth writing down.
